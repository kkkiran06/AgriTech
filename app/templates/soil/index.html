Organic vs Chemical Fertilizers: Balanced Farming

Organic Fertilizers (Bio-Inputs)

Includes Farmyard Manure (FYM), Vermicompost, Neem Cake, and Green Manure (Dhaincha/Sunhemp).

  • Improves soil structure, water retention, and microbial biodiversity.
  • Slow-release nutrients prevent fertilizer burn and groundwater pollution.
  • Maintains neutral pH and reduces soil salinity over years.
Chemical Fertilizers (Inorganic)

Includes Urea, DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate), MOP (Muriate of Potash), and Single Super Phosphate (SSP).

  • Provides high concentrations of immediate, plant-available N-P-K.
  • Critical during early vegetative flush and grain/fruit filling stages.
  • Must always be balanced with organic carbon to prevent soil compaction and acidification.
