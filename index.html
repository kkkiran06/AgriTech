🎯 The Problem We Solve

Indian farmers face critical challenges every season that impact their livelihood and food security

🌧️

Unpredictable Weather

Erratic monsoons, unseasonal rains, and droughts devastate crops. Farmers lose ₹1.5 lakh crore annually to weather events.

🦠

Crop Diseases & Pests

Late detection of diseases like rice blast, ragi smut, and coconut mite causes 15-25% yield loss every year.

🪨

Degrading Soil Health

Over-use of chemical fertilizers and poor soil management reduce productivity by 20-30% over a decade.

📉

Uncertain Market Prices

Farmers get only 25-30% of consumer price due to middlemen. No real-time mandi price visibility.

📄 What data do we collect? ▼
We collect only farm-related data you provide: location, soil type, crop info, and weather preferences. We do not access contacts, photos, or personal files. Phone number is used only for OTP login.
🔄 How is data used? ▼
Data is used solely to generate farm plans, weather alerts, disease detection, and market recommendations. Anonymized aggregate data may be used to improve our AI models. No data is shared with third parties for advertising.
🗑️ How to delete my data? ▼
Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete My Data. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. You can also email privacy@krishimitra.in. We comply with the right to erasure under India's DPDP Act.
👥 Who can see my farm data? ▼
Only you can see your complete farm data. Crop experts you approve can see limited field data to advise you. Suppliers see only anonymized regional demand data. KrishiMitra staff have no access to individual farm data.
